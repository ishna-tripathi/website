Cloudy with a chance of Exomoons: Hunting for transiting exomoons around a brown dwarf

Investigating potential exomoon signatures around the brown dwarf 2MASS J1047 using photometric light-curve and spectral data analysis. Gaussian Process regression is used to model stellar variability and test transit hypotheses. This research was presented as a published F1000Research poster.
